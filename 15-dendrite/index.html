Simulation 15 · Diffusion-Limited Aggregation

Dendrite

Wanderers that freeze the instant they touch — and the shape remembers every collision.

growth 220

Diffusion-limited aggregation (Witten & Sander, 1981): particles random-walk in from far away and stick the moment they touch the cluster. Because exposed tips intercept wanderers first, growth amplifies its own roughness into a self-similar dendrite — the branching of frost, electrodeposits, manganese moss, and the finest leaf veins, all from one rule.
