Crinkle Margin

An edge that grows faster than its frame must ruffle.

stiffness
spacing

Differential growth keeps a closed ribbon of nodes evenly spaced while repelling every nearby node and inserting new ones over time. Forced to fit more length into the same plane, the curve buckles into ruffles — the mechanism behind kale leaves and the corrugated folding of dehydrated Rhapis excelsa that lamm-mit cites as a blueprint for high strength-to-weight, shape-morphing surfaces.
