The Gray–Scott model runs two diffusing reagents — feed U, kill V — across a grid clipped to a leaf. Tiny rate changes flip the system between labyrinthine veins, dividing cells, and coral fronds: the same self-organizing cellular geometry and tissue density lamm-mit highlights as a target for moisture-responsive, load-bearing bio-materials.
