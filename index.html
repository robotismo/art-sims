Art Simulations · Vol. I

The Leaf Atlas

Eight generative simulations that grow leaf structure from first principles — an algorithmic answer to the lamm-mit leaf-inspired image generation collection, where MIT's Markus Buehler lab fine-tunes diffusion models on <leaf microstructure> to design bio-materials. Where they sample, these compute. Click any tile to run it live.

Art Simulations · Vol. II

Cosmos

Beyond the leaf. The first GPU-driven pieces — millions of particles advected on the graphics card, where the same handful of velocity fields that move smoke, chaos, and galaxies are made visible at once.

Art Simulations · Vol. III

Emergence

Order without a designer — three studies where elaborate structure falls out of a few local rules: a lightning fractal, a fluid you can stir, and a flock with a hawk. Forged with Opus 4.8 · ultracode.

Art Simulations · Vol. IV

Magnum Opus

The crown. One piece that gathers everything the series learned — divergence-free flow, velocity-streak rendering, an evolving timeline — into a living tapestry of millions of light-filaments that breathes through its movements and never repeats. Forged with Opus 4.8.

Why these five. Each maps an algorithm family onto a design principle the lamm-mit work targets — venation hierarchy, cellular tissue, corrugated folding, vascular transport, and Fibonacci leaf arrangement. Every piece is a single self-contained HTML file: vanilla canvas, no libraries, no build step. Open one and read its source.
