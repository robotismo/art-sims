Stable Fluids

Ink remembers every breath of the air it drifts through.

A faithful run of Jos Stam's Stable Fluids solver: each frame the velocity field is diffused, made incompressible by a Gauss–Seidel pressure projection, then carried forward by semi-Lagrangian advection — backtracing where each parcel came from and bilinearly sampling it. A dye is swept along the same field. Vorticity confinement feeds energy back into the small swirling filaments that advection would otherwise smear away — the same eddies you see when ink unfurls into still water.
