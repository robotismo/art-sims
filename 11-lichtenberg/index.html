Where charge cannot rest, it finds the steepest path and burns it into a tree.

growth 9

The dielectric breakdown model (Niemeyer–Pietronero–Wiesmann) grows a spark by solving the Laplace equation for electric potential around a conducting cluster, then letting it advance into a random neighbouring cell with probability proportional to the local field raised to a power η. The result is a stochastic fractal — the same branched Lichtenberg figure a real discharge etches into charged acrylic or across the sky.
