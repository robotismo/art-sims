Simulation 16 · Voronoi Tessellation

Areole

The tissue between the smallest veins, settling into the most even packing it can find.

areoles 300

In a real leaf, the finest veins enclose tiny patches of tissue called areoles. Scatter seed points and assign every pixel to its nearest one and you get a Voronoi diagram — cells (areoles) divided by edges (the minor vein network), computed fast with the jump-flooding algorithm. Lloyd relaxation then drifts each seed to its cell's centroid, and the patchwork settles into the even, space-filling mosaic leaves actually grow.
