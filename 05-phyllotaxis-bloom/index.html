Simulation 05 · Phyllotaxis

Phyllotaxis Bloom

One angle decides whether a plant packs tight or wastes space.

divergence 137.50°

Each new primordium is placed one fixed turn from the last and pushed outward by √n (Vogel's model). At the golden angle 137.5° — derived from the irrational golden ratio — units never align into spokes, so they pack with no gaps and Fibonacci spiral arms appear. Nudge the slider a fraction of a degree and the packing collapses: the efficiency principle behind leaf arrangement and seed heads.
