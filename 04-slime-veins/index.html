Slime Veins

Forty thousand cells, no leader, one network.

Each agent is a Physarum polycephalum cell: it samples a chemical trail at three sensors, steers toward the strongest, moves, and lays its own trail, which then diffuses and decays. From these blind local rules a transport vein network self-organizes and prunes itself for efficiency — the emergent vascular logic lamm-mit's materials borrow for distributing load and moisture.
