Simulation 14 · Lindenmayer System

Frond

A single growth rule, rewritten into itself, unfurls a whole plant.

angle 23°
depth 5

An L-system (Lindenmayer, 1968) starts from one symbol and rewrites it with a fixed production rule — here X → F+[[X]−X]−F[−FX]+X — a handful of times. A turtle then reads the resulting string as draw / turn / branch commands. Self-similar branching and leaflets emerge from pure recursion: the grammar of how real ferns and compound leaves grow.
