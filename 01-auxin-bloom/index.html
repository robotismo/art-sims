Auxin Bloom

Veins chase hormone sources until the whole blade is fed.

speed

Space colonization (Runions et al., 2005) scatters “auxin” attractors through a leaf silhouette; the nearest vein tip grows one step toward every attractor pulling on it, and attractors wink out once reached. Final stroke widths follow Murray's law — a parent vein's cube radius equals the sum of its children's — the same hierarchical plumbing lamm-mit's diffusion models learn to draw.
